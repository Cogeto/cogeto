# xl-vr02: the two language versions do not say the same thing

**Sides.** `a` from `mdr-2017-745-hr` page 23; `b` from `mdr-2017-745-en`
page 23. Article 10(7) of Regulation (EU) 2017/745.

**Expected.** `contradicts`.

**Reasoning.** Nobody built this. Reading the same article in both languages for
`xl-vr01` surfaced it: the Croatian authentic text obliges manufacturers to
comply with registration obligations under Articles 28, 29 and 31, and the
English authentic text names only 29 and 31.

Under EU law both versions are equally authentic, so neither is the correction
of the other and neither supersedes the other. That is what makes it a
contradiction rather than a supersession: same subject, same slot, both current,
incompatible content. Rule 2 of LABELLING.md is satisfied on all four
qualifiers.

**Why it is the right shape for the corpus.** It is the only plain contradiction
in the whole vertical set that is not a document revision, and it is exactly the
kind a compliance reader would want raised and could not find by hand across two
175-page texts in two languages. If Cogeto cannot surface this, the bilingual
half of the claim is unproven.

AMBIGUOUS: it can be argued that this is a known translation defect rather than
a legal difference, and that a corrigendum has since aligned them. That argument
is the user's to make, not the system's: rule 0 says an ambiguous case is
labelled conservatively and flagged, and the conservative behaviour for a
compliance product is to raise the discrepancy and let a person decide. The
label is `contradicts` for that reason, and the counter-argument is recorded
here so a future labeller can weigh it.

**A note on difficulty.** The conflict is between two lists of article numbers,
not between two quantities, so the deterministic quantity arm cannot see it and
the judge must read scope from an enumeration.
