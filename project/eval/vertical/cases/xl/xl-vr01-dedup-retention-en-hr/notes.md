# xl-vr01: one obligation, two languages, one fact

**Sides.** `a` from `mdr-2017-745-hr` page 23 (case `hr-v001`); `b` from
`mdr-2017-745-en` page 23 (case `en-v001`). Article 10(8) of the same act.

**Expected.** `same_fact`.

**Reasoning.** The plan records that cross-language contradiction pairs do not
exist in the corpus at all, and that they prove the entity work from V2.3 item
6.1 rather than assuming it. This is the positive half: the alias mechanism has
to make the pair a candidate before any verdict is possible, so a failure here
is a candidate miss rather than a wrong verdict, and the harness prints those
separately.

The pair is real in a way a constructed one could not be. Both texts are
authentic language versions of one Union act, published on the same day with the
same legal force, so there is no question of one being a translation of lower
standing than the other. A Croatian medical device manufacturer holds both.

**Why a bilingual corpus needs this.** Without cross-language merging, every
obligation in a bilingual document set is stored twice, contradiction detection
runs over two disjoint halves, and a findings report claims twice the coverage
it has.

**Not ambiguous.**
