# xl-vr04: a revision arrives in one language first

**Sides.** `a` from `mdr-amend-2023-607-en` page 4 (case `en-v003`); `b` from
`mdr-2017-745-hr` page 89 (case `hr-v002`).

**Expected.** `supersedes_a_over_b`.

**Reasoning.** The realistic failure mode in a bilingual document set, and the
reason this pair exists at all: an organisation reads the amending act in
English, files the original regulation in Croatian, and the two facts about the
same deadline now sit in one memory in two languages, six years apart, with the
older one still marked current.

The pair asks for three things in sequence: fold the subject across languages,
recognise that the later document amends the earlier one rather than disagreeing
with it, and get the direction right. A verdict of `contradicts` here would be a
defensible failure, because handing the pair to a human is safe. A verdict of
`supersedes_b_over_a` would not: it would mark the 2027 deadline outdated in
favour of a 2024 date that no longer applies.

AMBIGUOUS: the spans are again not the same paragraph, the same objection as
`en-vr01` and `hr-vr02`, and the same answer. Recorded so the corpus's hardest
call is made once and consistently rather than three different ways.
