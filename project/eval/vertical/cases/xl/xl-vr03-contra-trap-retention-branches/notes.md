# xl-vr03: 10 years and 15 years are not a conflict

**Sides.** `a` from `mdr-2017-745-hr` page 23; `b` from `mdr-2017-745-en`
page 23. Both halves of Article 10(8).

**Expected.** `compatible`.

**Reasoning.** The counterweight to `xl-vr01`. That pair requires the alias
mechanism to merge an obligation stated in two languages. This one requires it
NOT to merge two obligations that are stated in two languages and are about
different device classes: 10 years for devices generally, 15 years for
implantable devices.

Everything superficial points at a conflict. Same article, same obligation type,
same unit, two different numbers, and the subject names are in different
languages so the entity path has to work twice: once to see that
"proizvođači" and "manufacturers" are the same party, and once to see that
"proizvodi za ugradnju" narrows it to a class the English fact does not cover.

A system tuned to make `xl-vr01` merge and nothing else will fail here, and a
system tuned to keep this apart will fail `xl-vr01`. That band is what the pair
measures.

**Not ambiguous:** the regulation states both branches in consecutive sentences.
