# hr-v002: prijelazne odredbe MDR-a, izvorni datumi

**Source.** `mdr-2017-745-hr`, page 89. The Croatian text of Articles 118 to 120
as enacted, the mirror of `en-v002`.

**Why this case exists.** The earlier half of the Croatian document revision
pair, and the Croatian date forms. "27. svibnja 2024." is a date written with an
ordinal day, a genitive month name and a trailing point, which is not the shape
an English-only date resolver expects. Whether the extractor keeps the date as
stated or normalises it is exactly what pair `hr-vr02` and the supersession over
`hr-v003` depend on.

**Labels.** Seven. Fewer than `en-v002` because the Croatian page carries the
same provisions in slightly less space and paragraphs 4, 5 and 6 of Article 120
fall past the page boundary; the excerpt is a page, not an article, and nothing
was trimmed to make it tidy.

**Not labelled.** The Official Journal footer and the full bibliographic
citation inside the Article 118 footnote.

**Verification.** `supported`.
