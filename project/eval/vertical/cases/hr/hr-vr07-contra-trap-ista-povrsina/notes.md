# hr-vr07: dvije stavke liste, dvije površine

**Sides.** Both from `nn-112-2017-jednostavne-gradevine` article 2 paragraph 2
(case `hr-v004`).

**Expected.** `compatible`.

**Reasoning.** Different subjects, so no conflict, and the pair would be
unremarkable except for one thing: both values are areas in square metres, and
the quantity parser has no square-metre unit. Every other numeric pair in this
corpus reaches the reconciler with at least one side parsed. This one reaches it
with neither, so it measures what happens when the deterministic arm from V2.3
item 6.1 has nothing to contribute and the judge is on its own.

That is worth a gate because it is the common case in Croatian construction and
planning documents, where areas outnumber lengths.

**Not ambiguous.**
