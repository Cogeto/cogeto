# hr-vr02: prijelazni datumi MDR-a na hrvatskom

**Sides.** `a` from `mdr-amend-2023-607-hr` page 4 (case `hr-v003`); `b` from
`mdr-2017-745-hr` page 89 (case `hr-v002`).

**Expected.** `supersedes_a_over_b`.

**Reasoning.** The Croatian counterpart of `en-vr01`, on the same two acts. It
is kept as its own pair rather than folded into the English one because the
Croatian date forms are the point: "31. prosinca 2027." and "27. svibnja 2024."
carry an ordinal day, a genitive month and a trailing full stop, and V2.0 item
3.4 recorded that Croatian relative dates do not resolve because chrono-node
runs in English only. A supersession decided on an unparsed date is decided on
capture order alone, which is a weaker signal than the documents give.

AMBIGUOUS: as in `en-vr01`, the two spans are not the same paragraph. The
Croatian pair is drawn one level closer, both being about how long a certificate
keeps a device on the market, but the same objection applies and the same answer
is given: the practical question is what a compliance reader asks.
