# hr-v005: Pravilnik o jednostavnim građevinama, članak 1.

**Source.** `nn-112-2017-jednostavne-gradevine`, article 1: the scope article
and its exceptions.

**Why this case exists.** The scope-and-exception shape, which regulations use
constantly and notes never do. Every substantive sentence here is built as
"iznimno od stavka 2." (by way of derogation from paragraph 2), so the fact is
only correct if the exception it belongs to travels with it. A fact that reads
"a water cistern of up to 27 m3 may be built without a building permit" and
drops "protivno prostornom planu" states something broader than the regulation
does.

It is also the case where the same 27 m3 cistern limit appears that appears in
`hr-v004`, under a different derogation. Those two facts are the same value
about the same object under different conditions, which is the dedup question
pair `hr-vr04` asks.

**Labels.** Six.

AMBIGUOUS: the two glavni-projekt exceptions in paragraph 4 are labelled
separately although they share a stem, because they cover different building
works and a reader looks them up separately.

**Verification.** `supported`.
