# hr-v008: obavijest o promjeni

**Source.** `ted-hr-2133-2025`, page 11, the change block.

**Why this case exists.** The Croatian counterpart of `en-v010`, with one
important difference: this change block carries real content. The English notice
says only "buyer correction"; this one states WHAT changed (technical
specifications, bills of quantities, draft contracts, and the submission
deadline) and WHEN the procurement documents were amended.

That makes it the corpus's clearest statement of the authority question item
6.4 raises and does not answer: when a later document says the technical
specifications have changed, everything extracted from the earlier version of
those specifications is stale, and nothing in Cogeto today reasons from a change
notice to the facts it invalidates. The pending authority-ranking cases in
`authority/` are built on this.

**Labels.** Four. Two are `decision` because a change notice decides something.

**Not labelled.** The header, the eSender contact block, and the version UUIDs,
which is the same exclusion `en-v010` makes and the largest single source of
precision loss on tender documents.

**Verification.** `supported`.
