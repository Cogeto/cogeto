# hr-v001: obveze proizvođača iz Uredbe o medicinskim proizvodima

**Source.** `mdr-2017-745-hr`, page 23 of the Croatian EUR-Lex PDF: the same
page as `en-v001` in the same act.

**Why this case exists.** Croatian extraction from a regulation, and the
Croatian side of the cross-language pairs. The two pages are the same
provisions, so a fact from here and its English twin are about one subject
under two names, which is what V2.3 item 6.1's alias machinery exists for and
what `xl-v01` and `xl-v02` measure.

**Labels.** Fourteen, mirroring `en-v001` one for one except that the English
page's Annex XVI common-specification obligation is labelled there and here as
part of the same enumeration.

**A real divergence, recorded rather than smoothed.** Article 10(7) reads
"registration obligations referred to in Articles 29 and 31" in English and
"obveze registracije iz članaka 28., 29. i 31." in Croatian. The Croatian
authentic text names one article more. This corpus labels each language version
as its own text says, because that is what the document holds; the pair case
`xl-v02` is built on this and is the corpus's one genuine cross-language
conflict that nobody constructed.

AMBIGUOUS: whether that divergence is a contradiction or a translation artefact
is exactly the judgement a compliance reader must make, and Cogeto's job is to
surface it rather than resolve it. The pair is therefore labelled
`contradicts`, and the reasoning is here so a labeller who disagrees can argue
with it.

**Verification.** `supported`.
