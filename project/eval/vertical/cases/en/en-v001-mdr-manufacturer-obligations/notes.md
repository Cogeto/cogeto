# en-v001: MDR manufacturer obligations

**Source.** `mdr-2017-745-en`, page 23 of the EUR-Lex PDF, verbatim reader
output. Articles 9 (common specifications) and 10(1) to 10(8) (general
obligations of manufacturers).

**Why this case exists.** It is the plainest thing the wedge claims: a
regulation states obligations, and Cogeto should hold them. It is also the
retention-period case, where one obligation carries a second branch for
implantable devices, so an extractor that flattens the two into one loses a
value a compliance reader needs.

**Labels.** Fifteen, one per substantive obligation in the excerpt. Each is a
durable requirement someone would later look up, which is the section 1 bar of
LABELLING.md. The two retention branches are separate labels on purpose:
10 years and 15 years are different facts about different device classes, and
merging them is a real failure this case must be able to see.

**Not labelled, and why.** The Official Journal running footer, the page
number, and the delegated-acts empowerment in 10(4). The last is the closest
call: it is a real provision, but it states what the Commission may later do
rather than an obligation with content, and section 1 excludes cross-references
with no content of their own.

AMBIGUOUS: 9(1) is labelled although it is an enabling provision rather than an
obligation, because it names who may adopt common specifications and under what
trigger, which a reader does look up. Labelled conservatively as a `fact`, not
as a decision or a commitment.

**Verification.** `supported`: every label is asserted directly by a span on the
page.
