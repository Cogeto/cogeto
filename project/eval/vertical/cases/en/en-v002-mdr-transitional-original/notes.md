# en-v002: MDR transitional provisions, the ORIGINAL dates

**Source.** `mdr-2017-745-en`, page 89. Articles 118, 119 and 120 (transitional
provisions) as enacted in 2017.

**Why this case exists.** It is the earlier half of the corpus's document
revision pair. Article 120(3) as enacted is replaced wholesale by Regulation
(EU) 2023/607 (case `en-v003`), so this excerpt holds the dates that a later
document supersedes. The pair case `en-vr01` uses facts from here and from
`en-v003`.

**Labels.** Eleven, all dated or conditional provisions. Dates are the whole
point of a transitional article, so every one of them is on a label with its
subject.

**Not labelled.** The running footer and the full citation of the Regulation
reproduced inside the footnote to Article 118, which is a bibliographic
reference, not a provision.

AMBIGUOUS: the two Article 119 and Article 118 amendments are labelled even
though each amends a DIFFERENT act, because each states a substantive change a
reader would look up. They are not part of the supersession pair.

**Verification.** `supported`.
