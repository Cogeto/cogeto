# en-v005: NIST SP 800-171 Revision 3, password management and withdrawals

**Source.** `nist-sp-800-171r3`, pages 46 and 47 (document pages 36 and 37).

**Why this case exists.** The later half of the standards revision pair. Four
requirements this document marks **Withdrawn** were live obligations in
Revision 2 (`en-v004`), including 3.5.8, prohibit password reuse. A withdrawal
is a decision that reverses an earlier one about the same subject, so this is a
supersession that carries no number: the pair case `en-vr02` scores it, and it
is the corpus's answer to "a genuine contradiction versus a legitimate
difference".

It is also the assignment-placeholder case. The document writes
"[Assignment: organization-defined frequency]" precisely because it declines to
state one. A fact that supplies a frequency is a fabrication, and section 4 of
LABELLING.md says so, so the label carries the placeholder in words.

**Labels.** Thirteen. Four withdrawals are labelled as `decision` because that
is what they are; the rest are obligations.

**Not labelled.** Running heads, the REFERENCES blocks, and the DISCUSSION
prose, including the sentence arguing that enforced composition rules provide
marginal security benefit. That sentence is a rationale for the requirement, not
a requirement, and it is the mirror of the exception made in `en-v004`.

AMBIGUOUS: 03.05.10 is labelled "withdrawn and incorporated into 03.05.07"
rather than as two facts. The incorporation is part of the same decision.

**Verification.** `supported`.
