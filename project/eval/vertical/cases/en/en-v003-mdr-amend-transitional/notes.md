# en-v003: MDR transitional provisions, the AMENDED dates

**Source.** `mdr-amend-2023-607-en`, page 4. Regulation (EU) 2023/607, the act
that replaced Article 120(3) of the MDR and extended the transitional deadlines.

**Why this case exists.** The later half of the document revision pair. A
compliance reader asking "until when may a class III legacy device be placed on
the market" must get 31 December 2027 from here, not the 2024 and 2025 dates
`en-v002` holds. This is supersession across document revisions, and the pair
case `en-vr01` scores it.

**Labels.** Eleven. The three dated deadlines are separate labels because they
apply to different device classes; collapsing them into one "the deadline is
2027 or 2028" fact would be the failure this case exists to catch. The five
conditions in paragraph 3c are separate labels for the same reason: each is
independently checkable and a reader looks them up one at a time.

**Not labelled.** The amending wrapper sentence. "Paragraph 3 is replaced by the
following" is document machinery about the amendment, and the content it
introduces carries the meaning. The wrapper matters for supersession, which is
the pair case's job, not a fact of its own.

AMBIGUOUS: the first label is `decision` rather than `fact` because an amending
act deciding to replace a provision is closer to a decision than to a standing
fact. Flagged because a labeller could reasonably call it `fact`; the kind is
not scored by any gate, so the choice costs nothing either way.

**Verification.** `supported`.
