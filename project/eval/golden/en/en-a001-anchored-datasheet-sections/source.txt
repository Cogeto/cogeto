PWR-3000 Series Power Supplies
Product Datasheet, Rev B
Nordwind Elektronik GmbH, 12 May 2026

The PWR-3000 series covers two supply models for cabinet installations.

Model PWR-3100
Continuous output: 100 W.
Requires a ventilation gap of 30 mm above the housing.

Model PWR-3200
Continuous output: 200 W.
Requires forced-air cooling in enclosures above 35 degrees C ambient.
