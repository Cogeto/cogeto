# en-r011: a correction carries no dates

The ordinary shape of a human correction: someone re-states a number and says
they got it wrong, without naming a date on either side. Nothing in either
record marks a validity interval, so the only ordering signal the direction
guard can use is capture time.

The case exists because the failure mode is asymmetric. Read as a
**contradiction**, it costs a human a review of something they already
resolved. Read as supersession in the **wrong** direction, it buries the
corrected figure and answers with the mistake.
