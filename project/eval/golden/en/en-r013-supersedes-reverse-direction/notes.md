# en-r013: the newer fact is the second one

Every other supersedes pair in the corpus lists the newer record as `a`, which
is the house convention. A judge that learned position instead of time scores
all of them correctly and is still wrong.

This case is the same shape as an ordinary deadline move, with the two records
swapped. `b` is captured a week later, says so in its own text ("not 15
September"), and carries the later validity interval, so nothing but position
points at `a`.
